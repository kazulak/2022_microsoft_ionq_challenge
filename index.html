How to Play QuanTris

The Core Concept

In Quantum Tetris, blocks represent qubits. Instead of clearing horizontal rows like in traditional Tetris, your goal is to clear blocks by creating destructive quantum interference between the falling active block and stationary blocks already on the board.

1. Qubit Shapes & Grid Alignment

A block is comprised of a Qubit Center (a glowing circle) and an Arrow indicating its state. The block physically occupies both the center cell and the target cell the arrow points to:

  • Arrow Right (Cyan): state $|0\rangle$. Covers the center and the cell to its right.
  • Arrow Up (Blue): state $|1\rangle$. Covers the center and the cell above it.
  • Arrow Diagonal (Purple): Superposition state. Covers the center and the cell diagonal to it.

2. Applying Quantum Gates

Use keyboard keys or click the gate buttons to rotate/manipulate the falling block's state and physically change its shape:

  • X Gate (Key X): Pauli-X flips the state. (NOT operation, e.g., swaps $|0\rangle \leftrightarrow |1\rangle$).
  • Z Gate (Key Z): Pauli-Z flips the phase (negates the $|1\rangle$ coefficient).
  • H Gate (Key H): Hadamard gate creates/destroys superposition (rotates the arrow by 45°).
  • CX / CZ / CH: Entangle and swap states on 2-qubit blocks.

💥 How to Clear Blocks (Interference Examples)

To clear blocks, you must land your active block on top of a stationary block such that they occupy the exact same squares, and their state vectors are opposite (cancelling out), meaning $v_{\text{active}} = -v_{\text{board}}$ (out of phase by 180°).

Example 1: Cancelling $|0\rangle$ (Horizontal Qubits)

A stationary block is on the board in the $-|0\rangle$ state (arrow pointing Left $\leftarrow$). To cancel it, make your falling block point Right $\rightarrow$ ($+|0\rangle$) and land it on the same coordinates. They will cancel and both vanish!

Example 2: Phase Flipping for $|1\rangle$ (Vertical Qubits)

A stationary block is on the board in the $|1\rangle$ state (arrow pointing Up $\uparrow$). If your falling block is also in the $|1\rangle$ state ($\uparrow$), they will not cancel. First press **Z** to flip the falling block's phase to $-|1\rangle$ (arrow pointing Down $\downarrow$). When they touch, they will cancel!

Example 3: Superposition & Rotations

Your falling block points Up-Right $\nearrow$. Pressing **H** rotates it to point Right $\rightarrow$. By combining **H** (45° rotation) and **X** (90° reflection), you can rotate your qubit to point in any of the 8 directions to match and cancel a target.

💡 Pro Tip: Ghost Block & Block Outlines

Look at the **Ghost Block** (the dashed outline at the bottom of the grid) to see exactly which cells your block will cover when it lands. Align the shapes first using left/right move keys, then apply quantum gates until the active block's arrows point in the opposite direction of the target block's arrows.
